**Confidential manuscripts and research data**

We use non-public research materials only for the agreed project,
necessary administration or another purpose that is lawfully permitted
and appropriately disclosed.

Access is limited to people and service providers who need the
information for their assigned role.

Without separate permission, we do not publish or use identifiable

* Teaching or demonstration materials.
* Public case studies.

* Downloadable templates.
* Publicly accessible research resources.

Please tell us before sharing material subject to an NDA, institutional

**Research participants and sensitive information**

We do not ordinarily require identifiable participant data to provide
language editing, formatting or reference-management services.

Where possible, please:

* Replace participant names with codes.
* Remove contact details and unnecessary identifying information.
* Exclude signatures, identity documents and consent forms.
* Remove unnecessary medical, financial or other sensitive information.
* Keep the key linking participant codes to identities with your
research team.

Pseudonymised information may still be personal information if someone
can be reidentified.

Do not send identifiable patient records, children's information,
biometric or genetic information, confidential personnel records or
similarly sensitive material until we have agreed an appropriate
handling arrangement.

Your institution's ethics approval or a participant's consent to
research does not automatically authorise disclosure to an external
editor or AI provider. Please verify that the intended sharing is
permitted.

If we discover unnecessary sensitive information, we may pause work,
restrict access, request a redacted replacement or securely delete the
relevant material, subject to applicable obligations.

Some services may involve AI-assisted tools for activities such as:

* Formatting assistance.
* Visual-content development.

Where non-public client material would be submitted to an external AI

We aim to limit submitted content to what is necessary, for example by

AI-provider practices can vary by product, subscription, account
settings and contract. Depending on those arrangements, providers may
retain prompts and outputs, conduct security monitoring or permit
authorised human review.

We will not describe a workflow as "zero retention," "not used for
training" or "fully confidential" unless the applicable provider
arrangements support that statement.

We do not independently use client manuscripts or datasets to train or
fine-tune AI models without a separate, explicit agreement.

Where provider-side training or product-improvement use cannot be
excluded, we will explain this before submitting non-public content and
will not proceed without the necessary authorisation.

If your institution prohibits AI processing, notify us before work
begins. An AI-free workflow is available only where expressly agreed and
operationally feasible.

**DrillBit similarity and AI-detection services**

If you order a DrillBit report, the relevant document must be submitted
to DrillBit for processing.

The uploaded file may include its text, embedded information, author
details and metadata unless these are removed beforehand.

Processing may involve comparison with available sources, generation of
a similarity or AI-detection report, and storage according to the
provider's terms and the settings available under our account.

Before uploading, we will explain the applicable repository arrangement
and confirm any agreed restriction on repository submission.

**A "no-repository" setting, where available, should not be assumed to
mean that no operational copies, logs or backups are retained.**

We will not promise permanent deletion, no indexing or exclusion from
future comparisons unless those outcomes are supported by the provider's
current arrangements.

If you request deletion of a document already submitted to DrillBit, we
will take the steps available to us and explain any provider-controlled
limitations.

**How we protect information**

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate
to the information and the risks involved.

Our implemented safeguards include restricting access to client
information to authorised personnel who need it to perform their work;
using strong, unique passwords and multi-factor authentication on
accounts where supported; transferring files through HTTPS-enabled
services or other encrypted channels; protecting work devices with
encryption and automatic screen locks; keeping operating systems and
applications updated; requiring confidentiality commitments from
personnel and contractors who handle client materials; and restricting
access to backups, with encryption and scheduled deletion or
overwriting.

We also limit unnecessary copying and sharing of research documents,
review access permissions when responsibilities change, and securely
delete files according to our retention policy.

Access to project materials is provided on a need-to-know basis. Access
permissions are reviewed when responsibilities change or an engagement
ends.

No website, communication channel or storage system is completely
secure. We cannot guarantee that unauthorised access, loss or disclosure
will never occur.

If a security incident affects personal information, we will
investigate, take appropriate containment and remediation steps, and
notify affected people or competent authorities where required by
applicable law.

You should also protect your devices and accounts and avoid sharing
project download links publicly.
